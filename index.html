Getting started

What does AdZero actually block?

Requests to known ad networks, in-app ad providers, trackers and analytics services. The lists are built into the app, so blocking works the moment you switch protection on.

Why did my phone ask me to add a VPN profile?

Neither iOS nor Android lets an app filter domain lookups any other way. The “VPN” AdZero creates never leaves your phone: it only catches DNS requests, drops the ones that point to ads and passes the rest to a public DNS server. Websites, video and messages keep going over your regular connection.

How do I switch protection on or off?

Tap the shield on the main screen. You can also disconnect AdZero from the VPN section of your phone’s system settings.

Which DNS server is used, and can I pick another one?

Cloudflare is used out of the box. Google Public DNS and Quad9 are also available under Settings → DNS server. Turn protection off first — the server can only be changed while AdZero is disconnected.

Troubleshooting

A site or app broke after I turned AdZero on

Some services load their content from the same domains they use for ads. Add the site under Settings → Allowed sites and AdZero will leave it — and all its subdomains — alone. The Statistics screen lists the domains blocked most often, which helps to find the culprit.

I still see some ads

AdZero works at the domain level. When an app shows ads from the very server that delivers its videos or posts, there is nothing to separate them by, so those ads get through. Double-check that the shield is on, too.

Protection keeps switching off

On iPhone and iPad, enable Always on in AdZero’s Settings so it reconnects after a restart or a network change. On Android this is a system option: open Settings → Network & internet → VPN, tap the gear next to AdZero and turn on Always-on VPN.

AdZero disconnected when I opened another VPN app

That’s expected — your phone can run only one VPN at a time. Starting a different VPN turns AdZero off, and turning AdZero on disconnects the other one.

Privacy

Do you see what I browse?

No. AdZero has no servers and no sign-in, and the app contains no analytics. Blocking statistics are saved on your phone only; clear them with Reset statistics in Settings or on the Statistics screen. The full picture is in the Privacy Policy.
