Frequently asked questions

Why do I see a VPN icon?

AdZero uses an on-device VPN that only carries DNS lookups — the step where an app asks where a server lives. Your actual traffic goes straight to the internet and is never routed through our servers. We don’t have any.

A website or app doesn’t work properly

Open Settings → Allowed sites and add the site that’s being blocked. Allowed sites are never blocked and cover subdomains too. You can see the most blocked domains on the Statistics screen.

Some ads are still showing

AdZero blocks ads by domain name. Ads delivered from the same domain as the content itself (for example, in some video or social apps) can’t be blocked this way. Also check that protection is on.

Protection turns off by itself

On iPhone and iPad, go to Settings in AdZero and turn on Always on — protection will then reconnect automatically after network changes and restarts. On Android, turn on Always-on VPN for AdZero in the system settings (Settings → Network & internet → VPN).

How do I turn AdZero off?

Tap the shield on the AdZero home screen. You can also switch it off from the VPN section of your device’s settings.

Which DNS provider does AdZero use?

Cloudflare by default. You can switch to Google Public DNS or Quad9 in Settings → DNS server while protection is off.

Can I use AdZero together with another VPN?

No. iOS and Android allow only one active VPN at a time, so turning on another VPN switches AdZero off, and vice versa.

Does AdZero collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics and no AdZero servers. Statistics are stored only on your device and you can erase them any time with Reset statistics in Settings or on the Statistics screen. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
