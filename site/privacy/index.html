Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the AdZero app for iPhone, iPad and Android (“AdZero” or “the app”) handles information. AdZero is developed and published by D.K. BARAK BOOK KEEPING LTD. (“we”, “us”, “our”).

Summary

  • AdZero blocks ads, in-app ads, trackers and analytics domains by checking DNS lookups against blocklists built into the app. All filtering happens on your device.
  • We do not operate any servers for AdZero. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs (including no crash-reporting SDKs).
  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data.
  • DNS lookups that are not blocked are sent directly from your device to the DNS resolver you choose: Cloudflare (the default), Google Public DNS or Quad9. That provider’s own privacy policy applies to those lookups.
  • Blocking statistics are stored only on your device, and you can reset them at any time.

1. How AdZero works (the on-device VPN)

AdZero uses the system VPN interface — Apple’s Network Extension framework (a “packet tunnel provider”) on iPhone and iPad, and Android’s VpnService on Android — to install a VPN configuration on your device. When you first turn protection on, the system asks for your permission to add this configuration.

Although the system labels it a “VPN”, this tunnel runs entirely on your device. It does not route your internet traffic to us or to any remote VPN server.

  • Only DNS lookups enter the tunnel. AdZero sets a virtual DNS server address that exists only on your device and routes only that one address into the tunnel. All other traffic (web pages, app content, streaming, messages, downloads and so on) bypasses the tunnel and travels over your normal connection. AdZero does not inspect, modify or record that traffic.
  • Each lookup is checked on the device. For each DNS lookup, AdZero reads the domain name (for example, ads.example.com) and checks it against the built-in blocklists and your list of allowed sites.
  • Blocked lookups never leave your device. If a domain is blocked, AdZero answers the lookup itself with a “no such domain” response, so the ad or tracker cannot load. The lookup is not sent anywhere.
  • Allowed lookups go to your chosen resolver. If a domain is not blocked, AdZero forwards the DNS query to the DNS resolver you selected and returns its answer to the app that asked.
  • Blocklists are built in. The blocklists ship inside the app and work offline. AdZero does not download blocklists or any other content from us.

AdZero does not see or record the content of your communications, full web addresses (URLs), or any traffic other than the DNS lookups described above.

2. Information that leaves your device: DNS lookups

To keep your internet connection working, every DNS lookup that AdZero does not block must be answered by a DNS resolver. AdZero sends these lookups directly from your device to the resolver you selected. They never pass through us.

What the resolver receives

  • The DNS query generated by your device, which contains the domain name being looked up and the record type.
  • Your device’s public IP address, which, as with any internet connection, is visible to the server you connect to.

How lookups are sent

Lookups are sent as standard DNS (UDP port 53), the same way your device sends them without AdZero. Standard DNS is not encrypted, so network operators along the path, such as your Wi-Fi provider or mobile carrier, may be able to see these lookups.

We have no agreement with these DNS providers, we do not receive any data from them, and we do not control how they handle the lookups they receive. Each provider processes your lookups under its own privacy policy and may do so on servers located in other countries. Please review the provider’s policy before choosing it. You can change your resolver at any time in Settings → DNS server while protection is off.

3. Information stored on your device

AdZero stores the following information only on your device, in the app’s private storage. On iPhone and iPad this is a private storage area (an App Group container) that only the AdZero app and its own tunnel extension can access. None of it is sent to us.

Settings

Your resolver choice, your list of allowed sites and, on iPhone and iPad, your Always on preference. These are kept until you change them or delete the app.

Statistics

AdZero records statistics so it can show you how much it blocked:

  • Daily counters. For each day: the number of DNS lookups and the number of blocked lookups. Only the last 30 days are kept; older days are deleted automatically.
  • Hourly counters. The number of blocked lookups in each hour of the current day. They are replaced when a new day starts.
  • Most-blocked domains. For each of the last 30 days, the names of the domains AdZero blocked and how many times each was blocked, limited to 100 domains per day. Only blocked domains are recorded — the names of domains that were allowed and sent to your resolver are never written to storage.

Statistics contain no IP addresses, no full web addresses (URLs) and no information about which app made a lookup.

Other

  • The VPN configuration itself, which the system stores in your device’s settings.

On iPhone and iPad, like other app data, the information above may be included in device backups you create with iCloud Backup or on a computer. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple; we have no access to them. On Android, AdZero excludes its data from system backups.

4. Information we do not collect

D.K. BARAK BOOK KEEPING LTD. does not collect or receive:

  • Your DNS lookups, browsing activity, or the statistics stored in the app
  • Your name, email address, phone number or any other contact information (the app has no accounts or sign-in)
  • Your IP address
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, files or other content
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers (IDFA, Android Advertising ID)
  • Usage analytics or crash reports through third-party tools
  • Payment information (the app has no in-app purchases; any store purchase is processed by Apple or Google, and we do not receive your payment details)

AdZero does not show ads, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not use push notifications or cloud sync.

Platform diagnostics. If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple (through App Store Connect) or Google (through Google Play Console) may provide us with crash reports and aggregated usage statistics, under their own privacy policies. We use this information only to fix problems and improve the app.

5. How information is used

Because we do not receive your personal data, we do not use it for any purpose. The processing described above takes place on your device, at your direction, solely to filter DNS lookups, apply your settings and allowed sites, and show you your statistics. We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your data, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

6. Third parties

  • Your DNS resolver. As described in Section 2, allowed DNS lookups are sent to the resolver you choose.
  • Apple and Google. AdZero is distributed through the App Store and Google Play and runs on their operating systems. Apple’s and Google’s privacy policies apply to their services, including any device backups and diagnostics you choose to share.

AdZero does not include any third-party SDKs, and we do not share information with any other third party.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

All AdZero data is stored on your device and is under your control:

  • Reset statistics: tap Reset statistics in Settings or on the Statistics screen. This erases the daily and hourly counters and the most-blocked domains from your device.
  • Edit allowed sites: go to Settings → Allowed sites.
  • Change your resolver: turn protection off, then go to Settings → DNS server.
  • Turn off protection: tap the shield on the AdZero home screen. You can also remove the VPN configuration at any time in your device’s settings (on iPhone and iPad: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management; on Android: Settings → Network & internet → VPN).
  • Delete everything: deleting AdZero from your device removes all of the data the app has stored and its VPN configuration. Copies inside device backups you made earlier remain until you delete those backups.

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To ask about lookups processed by a DNS resolver, please contact that provider.

8. Children’s privacy

AdZero is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required by the laws of your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), including the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because we do not collect or store personal data about you, we generally have no personal data to provide, correct or delete. The data AdZero creates stays on your device, where you can view and delete it as described in Section 7. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information (as those terms are defined under the CCPA), we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information, and we will never discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If you have a privacy question or request, contact us using the details below.

10. Security

  • Filtering happens on your device, and only allowed DNS lookups leave it, sent directly to your chosen resolver.
  • Data stored by AdZero is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot access, and is protected by your device’s built-in data protection.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode.
